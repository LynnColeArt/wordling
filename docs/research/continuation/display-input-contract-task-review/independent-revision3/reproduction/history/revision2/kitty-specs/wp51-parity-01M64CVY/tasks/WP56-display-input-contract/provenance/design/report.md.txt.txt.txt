## Native versus external inputs

SDKWP11/PDF24 describes packet3 as document initial codes originating in setup; therefore initial state is not inherently external for every file. Explicit body codes and document-owned initial codes, where present and understood, can supply state. But absence in the selected minimal first image does not mean zero margins/tabs/paper, nor prove every corresponding default lives solely in SET. Missing values require the effective application/reference configuration and an understood precedence rule.

REF121–122/PDF138–139 distinguishes resource-derived character/space widths from display pitch for absolute-distance codes. Pitch is document-scoped and saved without a body code; Automatic/manual behavior cannot be reduced to a font-name label. The ruler additionally needs effective tab stops, alignment/type, origin/repetition and margin coincidence, plus the mapping to screen columns. Raw selected triangles at5-cell steps do not themselves prove that mapping.

REF699/PDF716 distinguishes general entry/display units from separately selected status units. Ln is paper-top distance and Pos paper-left distance;4.2 units have a specific exception. A screenshot with an inch suffix observes that state's displayed unit; it is not a recovered persistent setup selection.14 did not explicitly select/capture the Status Line Display setup choice. Pg additionally needs document page/numbering/section/format context, not token count or a visible row. Filename visibility is a separately documented setting, not a native filename field.

Private SET/PRS/font fingerprints establish which files were present, not their semantic values or a metric algorithm. Metadata may be compared; no raw SET/PRS/font tables or decompiled executable/provider is introduced. All72 complete native field coverage describes serialization, not a complete layout/display state.46's postprint counter02/read-only families remain read-only; their evidence does not fill the missing effective configuration.
