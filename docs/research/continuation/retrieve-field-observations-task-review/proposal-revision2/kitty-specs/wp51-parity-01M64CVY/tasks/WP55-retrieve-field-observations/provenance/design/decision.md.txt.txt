# Independent decision: acquire the second Retrieve field before editing it

**Design recommendation: a separate, prospective, original-only entry-field observation experiment is justified. It is not launch-ready or authorized here.** After actual WP54 approval, a separately authored task or explicit scope amendment may implement and independently review a maximum of four NEW failure-inclusive attempts: Insert r1/r2, Typeover r1/r2. Each performs one known-ASCII Retrieve → Save → clear → second Retrieve, observes a closed predeclared field hypothesis, then conditionally presses **F1 only** and exits normally. Do not type into, edit, or submit the second field in this first acquisition.

This removes the circular requirement for a preexisting photograph of a novel recalled field. The authority to form a testable prediction comes from experimental design; the authority for F1 comes from the documented Retrieve function and Cancel semantics plus paired direct original cancellation traces. Neither makes predicted contents/cue/cursor already qualified. A successful result qualifies only the observed entry/cancel history, after two fresh complete observations per mode. It does not solve every subsequent typed-field transition.

## Primary sources and applicability

The locally cached U.S. Reference manual is edition MNUS1WP51X1C-6/92, 61,124,991 bytes, SHA256 `ca028c9dd8527bddc88975225dcf9888dc85f765706ef3486e89502542f8f531`, 1048 PDF pages. It is later than the March 9 executable; actual original traces, not edition equivalence, settle this profile's behavior. The PDF was read locally; printed Cancel 55 and Retrieve 555 were visually inspected. Only metadata and our paraphrases are deliverables. Rendered private pages were removed, not archived or published.

- **Cancel, printed 55–56 / PDF 72–73:** F1 backs out of a menu or status-line request without retaining the requested changes. It is context sensitive: ordinary editing can invoke Undelete, Help does not close with F1, and macro definition handles F1 differently. Several menu levels can require several cancellations. Therefore this design permits ONE F1 only from the positively identified Retrieve filename request; it does not loop F1 until a normal-looking screen appears.
- **Retrieve, printed 554–555 / PDF 571–572:** Shift-F10 requests a filename; submission retrieves at the document cursor. A failed lookup redisplays a name for correction. A full pathname selects a file outside the default directory. These instructions identify the function and explain the separate error-return context; they do not describe successful-Retrieve recall contents, cue, hardware cursor or first-character replacement.
- **Exit, printed 195 / PDF 212; Save, printed 566 / PDF 583:** a displayed existing filename can be replaced by starting to type, or edited using navigation/deletion keys; Exit also documents Insert/Typeover. These are explicitly Save/Exit contexts. They motivate a later Retrieve-editing hypothesis but DO NOT prove it, and are not sufficient to authorize second-field typing in this minimal experiment.

Independently verified public sources are enumerated in `source-proof.json`: 62 actual Git blobs checked for blob ID, size and SHA. They include paired original13 captures/CRTC/VGA and actual archived scripts, approved39 exit, approved43/46 Retrieve/clear helpers and two identical original43 ASCII sources. Source13 is `18d6afe95ffcd676fc3f9e964dcad884c9a8c289`; source39 `419edc23d0739d490f2fd2c0094f5cc5eb0b1f80`; source43 `90ea1d4f4912e032061baccc6500f4d9ea232415`; source46 `44aab36accdf14a370a67fd73945907871ab7193`.

In original13 `session1.py:157–172`, each fresh iteration captures both empty and typed `B:\\MISSING` fields, then F1 and independently Escape. Both F1 returns have empty rows0–23, exact Doc1/Pg1/Ln1/Pos1 status, hardware cursor0,0 and display-start0. This proves cancellation of that source context, not a recalled field. Error-return has nonempty text WITH ListFiles and cursor at field start; a fresh typed field has NO cue and cursor at text end. These cannot be normalized into one state.

The bounded existing inventory inspected 1085 public screen captures under approved39/43/46 and found no intermediate Retrieve field. Approved `native_edits.py:166–167` uses substring wait → type → Enter → final capture. It is useful endpoint/trace evidence, not a safe classifier for the novel transient. Approved43 `native_genesis.py:180–188` observes clear-state transitions; approved39 `macro_quit.py:182–214` chooses exit keys from captured states. Their coarse state predicates alone are insufficient to authorize this experiment's next key: a new owned adapter must combine their actual approved mechanics with the full source/body/mode/frame checks below, without changing those helpers.

Source identity cannot be inferred from a filename prompt: it is the conjunction of fresh leaf/input pin, successful first retrieval trace, body expectations, actual Save completion, captured clear transition, and uninterrupted ordered history. Hardware row24 filename cursor is never a document token gap. Underlying blank body/gap is established before the field and checked again after cancel. No target field's stored recall text makes that document loaded.

## Closed prospective hypotheses, not qualified variants

The future plan freezes **one finite named table**, full 25×80 text+attribute planes and CRTC tuples for each mode. It must not implement a generic empty/nonempty classifier, optional suffix, arbitrary recalled pathname, cursor range, or Cartesian combination of cue/cursor variants. A compact recommended table is:

| Hypothesis | Exact field payload | Right cue | Hardware cursor | Reason for prediction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-empty | empty | ListFiles starts at col67 | row24,col26, word1946, display0 | successful history does not preserve a field value |
| H-retrieve-recall | exact prior **Retrieve input literal** | same col67 cue | row24,col26, word1946, display0 | Retrieve remembers its own last value and reselects it |
| H-save-recall | exact immediately prior **Save literal** | same col67 cue | row24,col26, word1946, display0 | filename entry shares the most recently completed file name |

Rows0–23 are predicted completely blank under this full-screen filename-field profile. Row24 is the exact Retrieve prefix, the declared payload if any, exact intervening spaces and cue; the terminating LF/text trimming contract is frozen separately from padded raw VGA. Recalled-name hypotheses borrow the *shape* of the witnessed error-return as an experimental prediction, **not its recovery history or observation authority**. Predict full attributes/CRTC from explicit source-backed cell assignments before launch; no masked unknown rows/styles. The table is illustrative design intent until exact literals/cell planes are reviewed and sealed.

This intentionally excludes a recalled value without a cue, cursor-at-end recall, basename-only recall, other paths, both names concatenated, modal/help/list-files screens and unexpected mode reset. Any could be real behavior; if observed they falsify this bounded table and halt the WHOLE plan. Do not widen the table after control1, treat unknown as an additional accepted variant, or spend an unused leaf to test a revised guess. Including multiple named mechanistic hypotheses prospectively is legitimate experimental design; using optional cue/text matching to permit operational typing would be a different and prohibited contract.

The next-key authority shared by these predictions is narrowly **F1 cancellation of an intact Retrieve filename request**. The manual provides that semantic authority without proving which hypothesis will occur. Exact first-use equality with an old recall photograph is therefore unnecessary. Conversely, a cue substring, blank first line or Doc1 substring cannot establish that function. No known-error-return path is treated as a fresh-recall witness.

## Completion, accounting and falsifiability

Freeze the four exact new IDs/ordered roster, all executable/imported helper and tool/profile/master/input/source/plan hashes, new private/public namespaces/socket/ledger and a new exact root grant before execution. First fault permanently halts the whole plan; cap4 includes setup failures and incomplete guests. Prior51 remains 1 incomplete/0qualified/7 permanently abandoned; historical46 remains13. Success requires two fresh complete observations **in each claimed mode** with the same exact hypothesis, field/VGA/CRTC result and cancel return. Agreement and qualification are after the fact, not prerequisites for making a prediction. A mode with only one completed observation is not reusable qualified authority. If repeated results differ, retain the difference and block reuse; do not pick a convenient variant or average them.

An unknown frame permits owned QMP containment only, not F1/normal-DOS qualification or failed-HD extraction. Physical safety release and behavioral completion remain distinct. Synthetic expected→classifier positives are not original evidence. No extra guest is needed merely to validate classifier code.

## Remaining limits and topology

This experiment can settle successful-history field **entry and F1 cancellation**, including Insert/Typeover differences. It cannot prove what a later character replaces/appends, navigation/Delete/Backspace/recall editing, cue disappearance after a recalled value, or Enter's exact native effect. After actual matching observations are independently reviewed, a future bounded proposal may form explicit replacement-versus-editing hypotheses against those entry states. Save/Exit's first-character rule alone is not sufficient; no typing permission or additional attempts are reserved here. Full H1/B still needs its separately authored native-operation evidence and grant; none follows from this design.

The actual54 offline checker remains Stage2 BLOCKED/0newruns until its own review. A new acquisition implementation should stay genuinely in E's field-evidence namespace, depending on actualapproved54 and declared13/39/43/46 source closure. A future native-E consumer can depend on that qualified evidence without E→X→E; adding an H/UI dependency to acquire these original-only facts would introduce E→H→E and is unnecessary. No topology is hidden by dropping a real helper dependency. Actual installed package/lane validation belongs to any later task amendment; this design authors no IDs or ownership exemptions.

**Disposition: proceed to separately reviewed task/amendment design for this cancel-only acquisition; no launch grant or operational/native permission.** All38 obligations/91groups/36lessons/nested/full M1 and M2 gates remain unchanged.
