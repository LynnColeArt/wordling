## F1 — Blocking: define batch versus leaf session and cumulative budget ownership

Clauses: E-API.md:5–11,34–40; ENVELOPE.md:3,13–26; GENERIC-SEAM.md:7,15,19,25,33; ACCEPTANCE.md:6,8.

The Controller runs the complete four-leaf batch and owns one Envelope. The envelope promises ONE P worker/session and ONE original child per leaf, forbids two sessions/newSession escape, and binds cumulative ceilings once at worker creation. After the terminal original, the same session may only run distinct readonly helpers; its original ProcessLease stays terminal. The call flow then proceeds to NEXT and another leaf. No clause states whether the960s/16 launches/4GiB/64MiB wire totals apply to one leaf or all four, how the next leaf obtains a legal new original session, or which live owner retains and allocates unrecoverable batch credits across that transition.

This cannot be implemented by interpretation: keeping the same terminal session contradicts the single-original contract; opening a fresh ordinary session would reset counters and origin unless a separately defined retained envelope actually binds it. Independent per-leaf budgets also change the currently advertised cumulative scope.

Preserve per-leaf physical release, whole-batch logical reservation and qualification-before-NEXT. This review does not choose the implementation or authorize a multi-original P session.

## F2 — Blocking: stop-join fact provenance is not specified

Clauses: E-API.md:3,13–18,28–30,38; GENERIC-SEAM.md:13–15; ACCEPTANCE.md:5.

join_stop(TerminalExchange, actual_P_ExitObservation, actual_E_stopped_proof) issues the nonconstructible StoppedRun, but its latter arguments are named as facts. P ExitObservation is deliberately an immutable, constructible observation, not a stop receipt or authority. Merely requiring an exact type, matching fields, hashes, or JSON rejection cannot distinguish an actual finish result from a same-type reconstruction or a replay from another operation. The design binds TerminalExchange to Controller/ProcessLease, but never states that join_stop itself invokes passive finish on that retained exact lease, or checks an issuer-held one-use result/provenance record captured by its own source-bound finish/stop operations. The E stopped proof and normal_DOS_witness likewise need explicit issuer/leaf/operation provenance and consumption rules.

Generic seal_stopped correctly avoids this problem by checking server history rather than accepting an ExitObservation. That does not repair the separate E authority claim: an authentic readonly P artifact still cannot manufacture original normalDOS/quit/stopped qualification. The prose intention is sound; the issuance rule is incomplete.

Minimal required revision: make StoppedRun issuance an operation on the retained Controller/ProtocolPeer authority that performs/captures actual finish and E stopped observation itself, or specify an issuer-private exact-object provenance record produced solely by those real operations. Bind leaf/ticket, source family, session/channel generation, ProcessLease identity/epoch, terminal exchange, earlier normalDOS witness and actual stopped resource; reject cross-leaf, replayed, reconstructed, already-consumed and lost-result input. Facts may remain diagnostics. No new P receipt constructor or private field access is needed or approved. Add conformance cases for same-type fabricated ExitObservation and copied/stale E stopped facts, not just JSON forgery.
