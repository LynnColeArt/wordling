## Why a closed layout/emitter is still blocked

A selected resource/font label plus WPU fields does not independently derive the
complete glyph advances/quantization/vertical origin, automatic rewrite/boundary
scheduling and initialization/termination/spacing behavior for arbitrary new text.
Existing opaque resource/cache/PRS relationships cannot be synthesized by guessed
zero/percentage/DPI meanings, a generic provider, or a copied PRN. The table does
not parse the private printer driver or decompile original code. Complete byte
examples constrain a future separately architected renderer; they do not define
one. Physical/historical-DOS/graphics/preview/font/locales and broader selection
branches remain unqualified.

No Stage2 is proposed. The known elementary contrasts already have paired
original observations. This task has not found a remaining at-most-four-attempt
experiment whose competing predictions would establish the missing complete
selected resource/stream/format derivation. Repeating normal success, choosing a
new arbitrary text or measuring more LF padding cannot establish that complete
algorithm; it would invent a probe without a discriminating decision. A later
specific resource/field hypothesis may justify a separately reviewed bounded
plan, not automatic guest work here. There is no orchestration/stop simulator or
fake unknown-classifier test to claim. Any future proposal must compose actual
39/43 helpers, reject the actual centred-modal+Doc1-status+blank-line negative
before another key, seal transitive inputs/custody and receive separate root grant.
No maximum4 budget is used or implicitly granted. New prints cannot qualify native
editing, and no previous failed/foreign original image is read for this research.
