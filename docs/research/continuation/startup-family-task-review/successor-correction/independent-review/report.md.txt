Adding exactly MutationResult.successor and Snapshot.successor repairs that defect without adding a method or changing method return shapes. The explicit facts-only exception for THESE TWO result types is essential: immutable scalar observations stay facts, while the one additional field holds a real issuer-created opaque object.
