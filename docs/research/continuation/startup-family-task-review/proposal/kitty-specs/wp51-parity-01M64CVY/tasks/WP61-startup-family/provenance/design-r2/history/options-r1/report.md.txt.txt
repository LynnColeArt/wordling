## Public contracts that the next design must actually settle

Streaming source capture returns immutable digest/size/identity facts, NOT large
payload bytes. Preserve existing ArtifactSpec.capture/verify byte-returning8MiB
ceiling and old Limits defaults unchanged. Introduce a DISTINCT opt-in streaming
policy with finite file/aggregate/FD/chunk/deadline/RSS caps and source-version
checks. The minimum declared image extent is64MiB; choose/justify the new maximum
and combined boot/image allowance in design review, without asserting unknown tool
sizes fit it. Tool identity/source hashing also needs a streaming path if a known
actual tool exceeds8MiB; do not omit declared sources or silently widen ToolSpec.
No cp/chunk-splitting wrapper bypass or full-image allocation.

PreparedWorkspace/MutableResource are proposed NEW capabilities, not current API.
An immutable master lease and freshly owned no-clobber destination must have separate
identities. A versioned mutable lease authorizes ONLY the explicitly declared working
file/tool operation; retain before/after identity/source facts and bounded new digest.
An interrupted/failed mutation yields dirty/partial/unusable facts, never rollback,
requalification or fresh ownership by caller JSON. Do not mark a deliberately
mutable image as immutable then swallow SourceChanged. Generic code never decodes
or grants validity to native/SET/PRS content; E owns interpretation and grant binding.

Managed foreground lifetime is a NEW operation, not a larger old run timeout.
The old command15s/readiness<=60s bounded by command, output1MiB per stream/16MiB
aggregate, RSS/address-space256MiB, processes8 and serial/main-thread constraints
remain its defaults. A new managed-lease policy must independently freeze finite
startup/readiness/total-lifetime/deadman/cleanup bounds, and preserve equivalent
stream/process/identity accounting continuously while control is yielded. Existing
E's900s per-leaf ceiling is a declaration, NOT permission or proof of a new generic
limit. No unlimited lease/callback, watchdog gap or counter reset at handoff.

A dedicated private supervisor process/channel is a credible mechanism for draining
and enforcing resource/lease expiry while E runs its own bounded protocol. It must
retain the child creation/PID/startticks/exe/source provenance and positive pidfd
ownership. Handoff capabilities cannot be forged by PID/pidfile/JSON or rebound public
wrappers. Every readiness/IO/acceptance boundary retains I1 supervision and I2 live
identity proof. Parent loss, unknown launch, source/owner drift or failure must retain
UNKNOWN/partial state and refuse further action; containment is never normalDOS.
Mechanism is a design candidate, not existing code or approval of subreaper/IPC details.

Keep daemon adoption OUT of the initial foreground capability unless independent
design establishes causal ownership from actual creation, not a pidfile assertion.
Old -daemonize recipes are NOT automatically supported. Future E must explicitly
review/freeze a foreground recipe OR require a separately qualified daemon handoff;
simply stripping -daemonize and using current run is not a working lifetime solution.
The generic lease never certifies normal quit: E still owns approved peer/account/
opaque quit receipt, normalDOS, stopped exports, master checks, per-leaf physical
release before audit and whole-batch logical reservation, plus exact NEW ROOT grant.
No P import/dependency on E and no copied full original controller.
