Bold on/off

     Bolds text on the screen and the printed page.  While Bold is on, all
     typed text will be bolded.  Press Bold again to turn bolding off.

     To bold existing text, Block the text and press Bold.

Note: When Bold is on, the position number on the status line is bolded as a
     reminder.
