Search

     Searches forward (F2) or backward (Shift-F2) through your text for a
     specific combination of characters and/or codes.  After entering the
     search text, press Search again to start the search.  If the text is
     found, the cursor will be positioned just after (to the right of) it.
     Lowercase letters in the search text match both lowercase and uppercase.
     Uppercase letters match only uppercase.

     Extended Search
     Pressing Home before pressing Search extends the search into headers,
     footers, footnotes, endnotes, graphics box captions, and text boxes.  To
     continue the extended search, press Home, Search.
