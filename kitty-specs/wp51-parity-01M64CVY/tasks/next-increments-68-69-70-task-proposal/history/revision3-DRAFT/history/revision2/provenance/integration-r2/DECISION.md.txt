## F1 resolved by explicit smaller delivery

Choose the honest minimum: ONE leaf per Controller/Envelope/Session, exactly ONE original foreground process maximum. There is NO run_complete_batch, NEXT, subset, resume, repeated launch, successor-session constructor or batch envelope API in this delivery. Every numerical ceiling in ENVELOPE.md applies to that ONE leaf from its pre-effect origin through its final semantic audit/publication. Helpers stay inside that same worker/session and counters. Once terminal, the original never restarts.

The previously described complete four-leaf Enter family is DEFERRED, including its two fresh Insert and two fresh Typeover repetitions and any grant/accounting integration that spans them. A separately authorized single-leaf experiment could eventually use this primitive; this design authorizes none. No future coordinator may call open repeatedly under an existing four-leaf grant and claim cumulative enforcement. Actual multi-leaf acquisition needs a separately reviewed retained batch issuer/credit protocol (absolute batch origin, reservations, lost-result spending, per-leaf issuer, no refund/reset, qualification-before-NEXT), or an explicitly reviewed different budget/grant contract. Neither exists here. This incremental boundary retains the full mission obligations; it does not declare their repetition proof complete.

## F2 resolved by internal stop collection

StoppedRun issuance takes no ExitObservation, stopped-fact dictionary, normalDOS boolean or public receipt argument. Controller.finish_and_collect_stop() itself calls passive finish on its retained exact ProcessLease, collects the E-controlled terminal/stop observations, and consumes issuer-held provenance records from its own earlier source-qualified normalDOS observation and actual quit wire operation. STATE.md and E-API.md specify the operation/leaf/session/source bindings and lost-result behavior. Public facts are diagnostics, never issuance authority.
