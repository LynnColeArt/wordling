    Intent: Material technical and governance decisions must be captured with enough context that future contributors can understand why a path was chosen and what constraints must remain true.
    Scope: Applies whenever implementation choices alter architecture, workflow policy, compatibility contracts, or long-lived maintenance obligations.
    Procedures:
